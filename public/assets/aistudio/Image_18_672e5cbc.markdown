## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the contemplative luxury and deliberate calm of the Japanese tea ceremony (chanoyu), reinterpreted for a sophisticated digital commerce experience. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
The brand conveys mindful opulence, artisanal purity, and heritage craftsmanship. The digital presence avoids hurried transactional pressure, instilling a meditative sensory rhythm that evokes warm ceramic surfaces, hand-whisked jade foam, and fine golden leafing.

### Design Movement: Organic Minimalist Luxury
The visual aesthetic balances high-editorial minimalism with tactile warmth. It rejects sterile flat design in favor of:
- Generous breathing room inspired by the Japanese concept of *Ma* (intentional negative space).
- Fine hairline rules reminiscent of gilded joinery (*Kintsugi*) and bespoke packaging seal lines.
- Subtle botanical silhouettes and serene editorial pacing, allowing high-resolution imagery of vibrant green matcha froth and natural ceramic vessels to hold center stage.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in ceremonial grade terroir: rich shade-grown tencha leaves, organic ceramics, and fine metallic leafing.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Deep Forest Green (`#173B2A` - Primary):** Serves as the anchor for supreme authority, primary CTAs, high-emphasis text, and dark ceremonial backgrounds.
- **Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B` - Secondary):** Used selectively for delicate emblems, subtle dividers, active indicators, and luxury badges.
- **Botanical Green (`#536B3E` - Tertiary):** Represents fresh tea foliage; applied to secondary button states, tags, and interactive hover highlights.
- **Soft Sage (`#A7B398`):** Serves as a softened tint for delicate borders, sub-card surfaces, and muted icons.
- **Warm Ivory (`#F6F1E6` - Base Neutral):** The principal canvas backdrop, creating a gentle, non-glaring parchment feel.
- **Cream White (`#FFFCF5`):** Reserved for elevated surface cards, modal dialogs, and illuminated image containers.
- **Charcoal (`#28291F`):** Used strictly for high-legibility body prose and detailed micro-copy.
- **Muted Stone (`#D8D0C1`):** Applied for subtle hairline borders, horizontal rules, and unselected component tracks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast elegance of classical serif display forms with the precise utility of a modern grotesque.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Editorial Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Headlines reflect bookish sophistication. Keep titles unhurried with generous line heights. Avoid heavy bold weights; allow character forms to breathe.
- **Functional Body & Interface (`Inter`):** Renders all nutritional information, ingredients, commerce utilities, and descriptive prose. Line-heights remain open to evoke spacious ease.
- **Pre-headers & Badges (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with broad letter spacing (`0.18em`) to mirror luxury packaging stamps and origin inscriptions (e.g., "UJI, KYOTO • CEREMONIAL GRADE").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces expansive margins and strict alignment, reminiscent of architectural tea house framing (*shoji* screens).

### Layout Structure & Grid
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid constrained to a maximum width of 1440px. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` with broad canvas margins of `4rem` to preserve perimeter quietude.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Complex side-by-side modules collapse into stacked vertical cards.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical pacing prioritizes generous section gaps (`space-xl`), allowing high-fidelity visuals of matcha preparations to command focus without competition.
- Product listings utilize asymmetrical arrangements and offset imagery to create an elevated editorial catalog feel rather than a dense shopping grid.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through organic tonal contrast and razor-thin borders rather than harsh drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas:** `#F6F1E6` (Warm Ivory) sets the foundational warmth.
- **Card Tier 1:** `#FFFCF5` (Cream White) lifts products and content groupings with an ultra-soft, warm ambient diffusion: `0 8px 30px rgba(40, 41, 31, 0.04)`.
- **Card Tier 2 (Hover/Active Modals):** Elevated using `0 16px 40px rgba(23, 59, 42, 0.08)` coupled with an illuminated border.

### Linework & Dividers
- Content divisions rely on 1px hairline rules using `#D8D0C1` (Muted Stone) or an accent line of `#C6A15B` (Champagne Gold at 40% opacity).
- Deep forest hero sections invert the stack: deep emerald backgrounds (`#173B2A`) layered with translucent botanical overlays (`rgba(255, 252, 245, 0.06)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored silhouette. Elements utilize soft, nearly imperceptible corner rounding (`0.25rem`) that respects Japanese joinery and ceramic minimalism.

### Form Semantics
- **Containers & Cards:** Styled with subtle `0.25rem` corners to retain clean architectural lines.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Use subtle `0.25rem` radius; strictly avoid rounded or bubbly pill shapes to preserve an understated luxury aesthetic.
- **Circular Emblems:** Seal badges, cultivar origin medallions, and tea-bowl avatar frames utilize complete geometric circles (`50%`), echoing the golden circular seal from the brand monogram.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Forest Green (`#173B2A`) fill, Cream White (`#FFFCF5`) text in uppercase Inter with 0.12em letter spacing. Padding: `14px 32px`. Hover state shifts background to Botanical Green (`#536B3E`) with a gentle transition.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent fill, bounded by a 1px solid hairline of `#173B2A` or `#C6A15B`. Text matches border color. Hover reveals a delicate `#FFFCF5` background lift.
- **Tertiary Text Action:** Monoline link underscored with a 1px Champagne Gold rule positioned 4px beneath the text baseline.

### Chips & Origin Tags
- Rendered in Warm Ivory (`#F6F1E6`) or Soft Sage tint (`#A7B398` at 15% opacity).
- Framed with an ultra-thin 1px border in `#A7B398`.
- Text styled in `label-caps` using `#173B2A`.

### Cards & Product Showcases
- Surface: Cream White (`#FFFCF5`) framed with a 1px hairline in `#D8D0C1`.
- Imagery: Set against warm organic backdrops featuring natural shadows and soft botanical light.
- Product metadata highlights origin (e.g., "HARVEST: FIRST FLUSH", "TERROIR: WAZUKA") placed above product names in `label-caps` in `#C6A15B`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** Minimalist underline style or crisp light-filled boxes (`#FFFCF5`) with a 1px border in `#D8D0C1`. Focused states swap the border to `#173B2A` with zero outer glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted 16px geometric squares or circles. Active state fills with `#173B2A` and displays an interior micro-check in Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`).

### Dividers & Ritual Elements
- Horizontal dividers consist of 1px rules colored `#D8D0C1`.
- Featured section dividers may incorporate a centered botanical leaf emblem or a miniature gold crest mark (`#C6A15B`) to punctuate the editorial flow.
