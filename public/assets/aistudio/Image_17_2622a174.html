CHAPTER ONE

A Ritual of Simplicity

Kavumi Matcha was created around a simple idea: everyday rituals deserve thoughtful attention.

Matcha offers a distinctive way to experience tea. Its preparation encourages you to pause, focus on the process, and appreciate the moment in front of you.

Our brand reflects that philosophy through an elegant visual identity, considered details, and a modern approach to enjoying matcha.

TERROIR & ORIGIN

Grown in Mist, Ground on Granite

Our cultivars flourish in Kyoto Prefecture along the pristine river basins of Wazuka and Uji. Dense morning fog provides natural shade before bamboo straw screens filter out 90% of sunlight, concentrating chlorophyll and sweet amino acids.
